What does the 72-hour timer mean?

The tracker uses Jean Ropke’s 72-real-hour species timer as a planning estimate. It cannot see who triggered a spawn or your actual server cooldown. Other players and scripted missions can differ. “Got it now” logs a cooldown; “Already have it” only updates the checklist. Clear a mistaken timer below or use Undo.
