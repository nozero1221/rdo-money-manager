Step-by-step Cayo route + tips
  1. Start with Longfin. Go to the best outside loot location you scoped before entering the compound.
  2. Fill the bag efficiently. For solo runs, prioritize cocaine, then paintings if conveniently available, then weed, then cash. Don't cross the whole island for a tiny upgrade.
  3. Take the boat to the Drainage Tunnel. Use the Cutting Torch to enter the compound without the front-gate route.
  4. Keep the compound route low-contact. The easiest repeatable route is to avoid unnecessary confrontations and stay on the shortest stealth path.
  5. Office detour: grab El Rubio's office safe because that cash does not use loot-bag capacity. Take an office painting only if you still need bag space and it fits your route.
  6. Get the primary target, leave through the main gate, grab nearby transport if available, and head southwest toward the coast.
  7. Fast escape: go off the southwest coast and swim away from the island until the escape triggers. This is usually faster than traveling to the marked dock exit.

Elite focus: stay under 15 minutes, avoid restarts, keep the bag full, and don't fail the fingerprint section. Don't sacrifice a clean run just to save 20 seconds.

Which Security Contracts to pick

When optimizing time, favor Recover Valuables and Rescue Operation when available, then other short Specialist/Specialist+ jobs. Asset Protection forces a long defense timer, so it is usually poor money-per-minute. Some Vehicle Recovery variants also run long; skip them when your board has a faster option.

Every Security Contract permanently improves your Agency safe income in milestones. The safe tops out at GTA$20,000 per in-game day after 201 completed contracts.

Payphone Hits

After 3 Security Contracts, call Franklin for Payphone Hits. There is a 10-minute cooldown. Always read the in-game bonus condition before completing the objective, because the bonus is the important part of the payout.

Dr. Dre Contract

The 2026 repeat finale payout is GTA$900,000. The first host completion in the current weekly cycle is about GTA$1.1M. It is a longer block than Security Contracts, so use it when Cayo is cooling down or when you want a predictable large payout.
